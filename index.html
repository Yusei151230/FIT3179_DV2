Australian public libraries · 2020-21 to 2024-25

More than books

You might picture a library as a quiet place
to borrow books. But Australia's libraries are busier
than they have been in years, and they offer
much more: free programs for every age, computers and Wi-Fi,
and a growing collection you can use from home.

1,733 public libraries across Australia
160 million visits in 2024-25, in person and online
1 in 3 loans and uses is now digital
Everywhere More than books Online
01

There is a library near almost everyone

Australia has 1,733 public libraries.
Most sit along the east coast,
where most people live, but they reach far inland too.

71% of library service points are in NSW, Victoria and Queensland

Australian libraries hold a huge collection,
and they are busy.
Since COVID-19, more people are visiting,
and borrowing more than ever.

+57% in-person visits since 2020-21

Visits grew by 57%,
but borrowing grew by only 18%.

→ So what are all these extra visitors doing?

02

More than books

Books are still at the heart of the library.
However people also come for different purposes:
Wi-Fi, computers, meeting rooms and programs.

+125% Wi-Fi hours since 2021-22, the fastest growth of any service

Programs in particular come in many forms,
on different topics and for different ages.
With over 400,000 sessions a year,
there is probably a program for almost everyone.

+67% program sessions since 2021-22

Most libraries are on the east coast,
so you might expect most programs to be there too.
But per person, South Australia runs the most.

38 sessions per 1,000 people in South Australia, against 16 nationally
03

What happens outside the building?

Most people still borrow physical books.
But over the same five years,
borrowing online grew much faster.

+80% digital use since 2020-21, against +18% for physical loans

Borrowing is not the only thing moving online.
In some states, people now visit the library's website
more often than its building.

1.5× as many online visits as in-person visits in South Australia

Visiting is moving online, and so is what people borrow.
Ebooks, databases and streaming now make up
a growing share of library use.

34% of library use was digital in 2024-25, up from 25% in 2020-21
