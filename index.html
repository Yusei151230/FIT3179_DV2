Australian public libraries · 2020-21 to 2024-25

More than books

You might picture a library as a quiet place to borrow books. But Australia's libraries are busier than they have been in years, and they offer much more: free programs for every age, computers and Wi-Fi, and a growing collection you can use from home.

1,733 public libraries across Australia
160 million visits in 2024-25, in person and online
1 in 3 loans and uses is now digital
Everywhere More than books Online
01

There is a library near almost everyone

Australia has 1,733 public libraries. Most sit along the east coast,
where most people live, but they reach far inland too.

Australian libraries hold a huge collection, and they are busy.
Since COVID-19, more people are visiting, and borrowing more than ever.

The number of visitors increases by 50% but why is the increase ratio of borrowing not proportional to that?
02

More than books

Books are still at the heart of the library.
However people also come for different purposes.

Programs in particular come in many forms, on different topics and for different ages. With over 400,000 sessions a year, there is probably a program for almost everyone.

And people really do come. Across Australia, library programs drew over 7 million attendances in a year.

03

What happens outside the building?

Most people still borrow physical books. But over the same five years, borrowing online grew much faster: ebooks, audiobooks, databases and streaming.

Borrowing is not the only thing moving online. In some states, people now visit the library's website more often than its building.

Visiting is moving online, and so is what people borrow.
