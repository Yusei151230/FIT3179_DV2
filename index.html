Australian public libraries · 2020-21 to 2024-25

More than books

Fewer Australians hold a library card than five years ago, yet more people than ever are using their library. You might picture a library as a place to borrow books. Here is what libraries offer today, and why you don't need a card to use most of it.

1,733 public libraries across Australia
160 million visits in 2024-25, in person and online
1 in 3 loans and uses is now digital

01

There is a library near almost everyone

Australia has 1,733 public libraries. Most sit along the east coast, where most people live, but they reach far inland too.

People came back, and kept coming

In-person visits rose 57% in five years, to about 90 million. Loans of books and other items grew 18%, even though there are no more items on the shelves. Each item is being borrowed more often. 2020-21 was affected by COVID-19 lockdowns, so part of this rise is people returning.

The number of visitors increases by 50% but why is the increase ratio of borrowing not proportional to that?
02

More than books

Books are still at the heart of the library.
However people also come to use the Wi-Fi, book a meeting room or join a program.

A program for almost everyone

Libraries ran more than 430,000 program sessions in 2024-25. Reading and learning is the largest type, but most sessions are about other things: hobbies, wellbeing, digital skills and community life. The largest group is not adults but babies and toddlers, with their parents.

7.2 million attendances across the country

People join programs in every state. Victoria had the most attendances, followed by New South Wales and Queensland. The size of each circle shows the number of attendances in that state.

03

What happens outside the building?

Borrowing moved online, but visiting did not. Digital borrowing (ebooks, databases and streaming) grew 80% in five years, far faster than physical loans. Visits went the other way: in-person visits grew faster than website visits.

Some states visit online more than in person

In two states, more people visited their library online than in person. In South Australia, people made 6.6 visits per person to the library website and catalogue, compared with 4.3 visits to the building.

One in three uses is now digital

In 2020-21, about 23 in every 100 loans and uses were digital. By 2024-25 it was 34. Move the slider to step through each year.

The digital shift is not the same everywhere

In the Australian Capital Territory, 44% of library use is digital. Victoria is the lowest at 29%. Hover over a state to see its exact share.
